My Kids TV is an open-source, ad-free replacement home screen and screensaver for Google TV, Android TV, Fire TV, LG webOS, and Samsung Tizen designed specifically for children and parents.

Instead of algorithmic rows, autoplaying trailers, and promotional banners, My Kids TV shows one calm, age-graded film or series recommendation at a time from the streaming apps already installed on your TV, alongside a clean app shelf and weather clock.

Key features:
* Age-graded catalogs (4-6, 7-10, 11-13): 800 curated movies and series (licensed under CC BY 4.0) evaluated for age fit, lasting value, craft, and fun, with badges for Must Watch, Learn Something, and Kids Love It.
* Works with or without an API key: Works out of the box as a calm app launcher, clock, weather display, and screensaver. Add a free TMDB API key in Settings to unlock poster artwork and regional streaming availability.
* Parent PIN protection: All settings, age-band switches, app visibility toggles, and mode switches stay behind a 4-digit parent PIN.
* Clean mode switching: Switch between My Kids TV mode and the TV's original stock launcher from inside the app (via one-time local ADB setup, no root required). Includes an automatic safety watchdog in a separate process that restores the stock launcher if the home process ever crashes.
* Built-in sleep timer: Set a bedtime countdown overlay that returns the TV to the home screen and sleeps the display when time is up.
* Optional remote button blocker: Optional Accessibility service to block dedicated YouTube or app buttons on TCL, Haier, Hisense, and Sony remotes while My Kids TV mode is active.
* 25 languages supported: Full UI localization across 25 languages with RTL support.
* Privacy first: Zero ads, zero telemetry, zero analytics, no user accounts, and no Google Play Services dependencies. Network requests go only to TMDB (if configured) and Open-Meteo (for local weather).
