Free My TV is a fast, ad-free home screen for your LG TV: your apps on a glass shelf, the weather and the prices you follow, a day look and a night look, and its own screensaver.

It installs as an ordinary app. LG's home screen stays where it is, and nothing on the TV is rooted or changed. While Free My TV is open, hold a number key on the remote: from then on, that key opens it from anywhere. On a remote without number keys, it's in LG's app list on the home screen.

This is the release build: it takes your city from the TV's time zone. The movie and series picks need your own build with a free TMDB key; the project page shows how.

Open source, at github.com/simbasjungles/free-my-tv and freemytv.io.
